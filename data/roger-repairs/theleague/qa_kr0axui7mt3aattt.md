**RotoWire is the official depth chart of the league** — that's the source of truth the constitution names, and if RotoWire and MFL agree on where a player lines up, that's his position and there's nothing to decide.

The interesting case is when they disagree, and that's where the rule has a second half worth knowing: **you** pick which of the two positions he carries — MFL's listing or RotoWire's. Not the commissioner, not whichever source loaded first. You ask, the commissioner makes the change, and it takes effect **before the upcoming week's kickoff**.

That choice isn't a one-time lock, either. A player the two sources list differently is eligible at **both** positions for the entire season, and you can move him back and forth between them **as often as you want**. Ride him at tight end while the premium is worth it, slide him to receiver when your lineup math changes, slide him back in November. Every switch runs through the commissioner and lands before that week's kickoff, so the constraint is the deadline — not the number of times you've flipped him.

One fence: the only positions on the table are the ones **MFL and RotoWire actually list**. There's no third option to campaign for.
